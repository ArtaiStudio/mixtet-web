Demo · simulated mixer

Try Mixtet in your browser

A simulated mixer, nothing to install. On the left the bandleader’s iPad as a standalone hub – no Mac, everything the Mac app does: Mixer, Channel, Monitors, Scenes, Band and Concert mode. On the right a band member’s phone controls only their own monitor mix. Change something on one and watch the other follow.

◌ Sound and meters are simulated – nothing plays

Try five things

  1. 1

    You’re Tom (guitar). On the phone, turn the guitar down and the vocals up.

    ME = your channels, BAND = everyone else. Single channels are under “Individual channels”.

  2. 2

    Between songs, mute the instruments with one button – MG1 Instruments in Live actions on the iPad.

    With a controller just press Play. The phone shows muted channels as “muted in the mix – group”.

  3. 3

    Load the next song – Scenes › LOAD (tap twice), or at the top in Concert. Monitors stay put.

    The preview shows what will change. The phone on the right only notices the new song – its mix doesn’t move.

  4. 4

    The kick sounds boomy: on the iPad open Channel › Simple and turn up Clarity.

    Clarity writes the mixer’s real EQ – the Whole chain shows what it set.

  5. 5

    Say something into the band’s monitors – hold TALK TO BAND.

    On the iPad Mixer on the right, or at the bottom of the phone. The house can’t hear it.

What’s not in the demo

  
The demo is just a slice of Mixtet. This isn’t in it:

  
  • A real mixer and real sound
    Here the mixer is only simulated – in the app everything goes to the XR18 and back.
  • Setup guide and pairing
    Set up in an evening, real QR and PIN for phones, Allow on the hub.
  • Effects, RTA and backups
    The Effects page, the spectrum in Channel, mixer backups, importing scenes from X AIR Edit.
  • The iPad app
    The iPad as a hub is in the works (TestFlight). Today Mixtet runs on a Mac – an iPad joins it as Full mix.
