Privacy

Controller: Martin Aichler, company ID (IČO) 88306321, Litoměřice, Czech Republic, on behalf of the band KRAI. Contact: booking@krai.cz.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the form: email, which mixer you have, what you'd run Mixtet on, how many of you play, whether you use in-ears, and optionally your band name, country and a note. Plus the page language and where you came from (e.g. a forum, based on the link). The feedback form also stores your message, app version and operating system. We don't store your IP address or browser details.

Why

To choose bands for the Mixtet beta, send you the download link and arrange feedback. Legal basis: your consent. We don't sell or share anything for advertising.

Where it lives

The website and the answers database run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages and D1) under our account. No analytics or advertising cookies, no web fonts, no third-party forms. The only cookie, mixtet-lang, remembers the language you picked.

How long

Until the beta ends, 2 years at most. Then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion and withdraw consent any time – just email us. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority (uoou.gov.cz).
